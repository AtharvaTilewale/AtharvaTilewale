## About

I build computational tools for **protein design, structural biology, and molecular simulation** — the kind of software that sits between a biological question and an answer you can act on.

Most of my work falls into three areas:

- **Structure prediction & protein design** — deep-learning models (Boltz-2, AlphaFold, inverse folding) applied to binder design and structural characterisation
- **Molecular dynamics** — GPU-accelerated simulation with GROMACS, plus the analysis layer around it: PCA, free-energy landscapes, trajectory post-processing
- **Research tooling** — turning fiddly, error-prone manual pipelines into reproducible ones that other people can actually run

> **What I'm working toward:** pipelines that take protein design from idea to validated candidate without the workflow itself being the bottleneck.
